# Product Requirements Document

## Product Name

Devcomm Sales OS

## Purpose

Devcomm Sales OS is a B2B AI sales automation platform. It helps Devcomm identify target companies, research them, determine fit, generate personalized outreach and sales documents, and track the full pipeline from imported lead to booked meeting.

## Users

Initial users:
- Owner/admin
- Sales user
- Viewer

Future users:
- other B2B clients using the same platform white-labeled or multi-tenant

## Main Outcomes

The app should help Devcomm:
- find better leads
- understand each company faster
- generate personalized sales material
- track every prospect
- avoid forgetting follow-ups
- connect meetings to Outlook Calendar
- reduce manual admin work
- keep costs under control

## Main Pipeline

Imported → Researched → Qualified → Approved → Exported/Sent → Replied → Meeting Booked → Won/Lost

## Core Modules

1. Dashboard
2. Companies
3. Contacts
4. Campaigns
5. AI Research Queue
6. Generated Documents
7. Outreach
8. Replies
9. Meetings
10. Calendar
11. Analytics
12. Cost Control
13. Settings
14. Logs

## Devcomm Context

Devcomm is a French B2B training and consulting company. It helps companies improve:
- commercial performance
- operational performance
- communication
- team behavior
- training quality
- professional skills
- driving/professional mobility/road-safety-related training through Skills2Drive

Target personas:
- CEO / Dirigeant

- DRH
- Responsable RH

Tone:
